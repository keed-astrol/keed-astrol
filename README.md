## About

I am an Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at the University of Nairobi, interested in the intersection of **electrical engineering, computing, intelligent systems, and applied mathematics**.

My work involves designing and developing engineering systems that bring together hardware, software, mathematical modelling, and intelligent computation. I am particularly interested in autonomous systems, embedded architectures, machine learning, and the application of scientific principles to engineering problems.

I enjoy approaching engineering challenges from first principles, understanding the underlying mechanisms, and translating theoretical ideas into functional systems.

**Areas of engineering interest:**
- Autonomous robotics, perception, and intelligent control.
- Embedded systems, firmware development, and hardware-software integration.
- Scientific computing, mathematical modelling, and computational inference.
- Intelligent transportation and distributed information systems.
- Electrical power systems, industrial automation, and control engineering.

---

## Current Engineering Projects

My current development work spans intelligent systems, applied computing, and engineering software. These projects are under active development; a dedicated portfolio documenting their architecture, implementation, and technical progress will follow.

### 1. Blood Bank Management and Traceability System
Developing a digital system for blood bank operations, inventory management, and blood product traceability, with an emphasis on improving visibility and coordination across the blood supply chain.

### 2. Agribusiness Platform
Developing an agribusiness platform for Equity, focused on connecting agricultural stakeholders through digital infrastructure, market access, and integrated agricultural services.

### 3. Intelligent Commuter System
Developing an intelligent transportation system aimed at improving commuter information, coordination, and the management of public transportation services.

### 4. Scientific Inference Engine
Exploring the development of a scientific inference engine capable of reasoning from mathematical relationships, physical laws, and fundamental scientific principles. The broader objective is to investigate how computational systems can derive and evaluate conclusions through structured scientific reasoning.
